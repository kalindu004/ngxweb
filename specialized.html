Specialized Services

Certain engagements require highly specialized expertise, certifications, tools, or dedicated project resources.

NGX provides access to these capabilities through our carefully selected and vetted specialist individual partners or the business partners network.

Services include:

  • Penetration Testing / Ethical Hacking
  • Social Media Account Recovery
  • Managed Detection and Response (MDR)
  • Digital Forensics
  • Digital Data Recovery
  • Major System Implementations
  • Dedicated IT Project Management
  • Advance User Training Programs
  • Software/Website Development
  • Artificial Intelligence Solutions

When you have a specialised IT & Cybersecurity need, we help you find the right partner, for the right price and manage that relationship and project end-to-end on behalf of you.
