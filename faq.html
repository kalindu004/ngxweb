FAQ

Is NGX a Managed Services Provider (MSP) for IT/Cybersecurity Services?

Yes, we are a professional MSP and more – We offer IT and Cybersecurity MSP services as well as Ad-hoc IT Project Management and Consultancy services.

Do you provide IT Governance, Risk, and Controls Related Services?

Yes we do! – IT GRC is embedded within the IT Department as a Service (ITDaaS) offering. You get an IT Risk Manager within the fully fledged Managed IT department that we offer.

Do you offer dedicated IT team outsourcing?

No, while we don’t offer traditional HR outsourcing of your entire team. NGX provides a more strategic and optimized alternative for outsourcing. We specialize in IT-Department as a Service (ITDaaS). Unlike standard outsourcing, which simply hosts dedicated personnel for within our company, our ITDaaS model gives you access to a fully managed, scalable IT department tailored to your specific and changing needs.

Do you provide IT Audit Services?

No, we do not provide IT Audit Services. -but we can help you find an independent IT Audit partner.
