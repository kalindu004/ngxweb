The NGX-Verified™ Program

NGX-Verified™ is an assurance program by NGX that publicly recognizes entities demonstrating strong cybersecurity practices and sound technology governance. Entities such as clients and partners are independently assessed against an NGX-defined criteria to determine their assurance level.

Security Assessments, Policy, & Risk

Our IT Gap Analysis & Risk Assessment service is designed to provide a comprehensive evaluation of your IT systems, processes, and governance. By identifying discrepancies between existing IT setups and specific business objectives, we provide actionable insights to your organization to help make informed decisions and strategic improvements.

  • Define Objectives and Scope
  • Collect and Review Current IT Information
  • Identify Requirements and Standards
  • Conduct Gap Analysis
  • Assess Risks of Identified Gaps
  • Develop Recommendations
  • Create Implementation Plan
  • Report Findings and Recommendations

Proven Tools and Methodologies

Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Generative Artificial Intelligence (AI) is utilized to verify facts and create reports that are well-structured and easy to understand for non-technical staekholders. AI can be used to predict certain outcomes of IT strategies and solutions, facilitating better decision-making.

SWOT Analysis

To systematically evaluate the Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats related to an organization’s current IT environment, helping to clarify strategic adjustments needed to enhance operational resilience and technological efficacy.

McKinsey 7-S Framework

To examine the alignment and interdependencies among the seven key organizational elements: strategy, structure, systems, shared values, skills, style, and staff, ensuring comprehensive organizational coherence in the IT strategy.

Fishbone Diagrams

To visually map out the causes and effects of various IT issues, allowing teams to systematically identify root-causes (potential risk factors and inefficiencies) that contribute to gaps in achieving strategic IT objectives.

Nadler-Tushman Congruence Model

To assess the alignment between an organization’s operations, including its IT systems, and its outcomes, by analyzing how well the components of work, people, structure, and culture fit together to achieve organizational objectives.

PESTEL Analysis

To assess external factors—Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, and Legal—that could influence an organization’s IT framework, ensuring that IT strategies are robust, compliant, and adaptive to the changing external environment.
